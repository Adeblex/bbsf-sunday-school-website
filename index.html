Our Identity

"...Sunday School Family!
Students and teachers of the Word!"

The Sunday School Family is a unit of the Bowen Baptist Student Fellowship, an arm of the Bowen University Chapel here at Bowen University.
We are a community of believers dedicated to growing in our faith and sharing the love of Christ with others.

A doctrinal powerhouse on campus, The Sunday School Family is one of the four teaching units of the BBSF.

The Vision

"The Gospel prevails in our days!
Great is the peace of the Lord in our time..."

The truth of God's word deeply rooted in the hearts and minds of people in Bowen University!

The Mission

...The Gospel to the ends of Bowen, the Gospel to the ends of the earth!...

To diligently teaching the word in and out of season, rightly dividing the word of truth.
